# Verbal identity

Read the approved positioning, audience evidence and product facts. Keep three separate outputs: **voice** (tone, vocabulary, examples and exclusions), **messaging** (value proposition, proof, message hierarchy and candidate taglines), and **story** (verified origin or founder narrative in short and long forms). Link every factual claim to a source; mark an invented illustrative scenario as fiction. Test English sample copy across named touchpoints without changing the underlying promise.

This workflow currently writes and checks English copy only. The brand team owns the rules; campaign and marketing work applies them to specific channels. Proposed wording becomes an approved baseline only through the same brand decision path as visual changes.
