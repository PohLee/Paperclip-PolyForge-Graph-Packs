# Brand architecture and naming

In `brand-architecture-naming`, first pin the current brand family and decide whether the proposed entity belongs under an existing name, an endorsed name or an independent brand. Record the relationship and tradeoffs before proposing names. Write a naming brief with audience, English-language pronunciation, intended markets and channels, prohibited terms and any equity that must remain. Explore distinct semantic territories, then filter for meaning, memorability and family coherence. This workflow currently generates and evaluates English names only.

Screen each finalist for prior art in its category before domain or handle checks. Record each check as `verified`, `conflict`, or `unknown` with source and date; a free handle never implies trademark clearance. Return a versioned shortlist and review. A shortlist is a recommendation; changing an approved brand or adopting a market-facing name requires the authorized owner decision and any required legal review.
