# Competitive intelligence watch

Use only when a competitor, market or regulatory watch is explicitly requested or scheduled by the host. Compare a versioned prior snapshot with a new dated snapshot. For each material change, cite the primary source, observation time, what changed, source limitations and affected hypothesis, decision or requirement. Preserve `no material change` as a valid sourced result.

Publish a competitive diff that passes `validateCompetitiveDiff`. Flag material impact as `DecisionReviewRequired` for the CPO; otherwise record the unchanged finding and next review date. Never rewrite accepted scope, a frozen run, backlog priority or roadmap automatically. A new competitor feature is not proof of customer need or of the competitor's actual performance.
