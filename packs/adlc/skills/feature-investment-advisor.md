# Feature investment advisor

Use when the CPO compares build, defer, further validation or stop choices. Select a decision method that fits the evidence quality. With sparse data, use ranges and explicit assumptions instead of a precise RICE number. Compare expected user outcome and revenue or retention effect with implementation, maintenance, external dependencies, agent/model operation, support and opportunity costs. State timing, reversibility and what evidence would change the ranking.

Publish a versioned investment case with source-linked estimates, data quality, method, choices, recommendation and decision owner. `validateInvestmentCase` checks the minimum fields. The Product Manager recommends; the CPO records the authorized build/defer/stop disposition before the backlog or roadmap changes. Do not treat a score as authority.
