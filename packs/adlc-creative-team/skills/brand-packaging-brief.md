# Packaging design brief

Run `brand-packaging-brief` from an approved baseline, real product dimensions and channel constraints. Define protection needs, primary/secondary structure, information hierarchy by panel, logo and color use, materials, SKU variation and unboxing intent. Identify mandatory claims and labels by market with evidence; route legal and supplier questions to their owners.

The deliverable is a designer and supplier brief. Ask the printer or structural designer for the dieline, material tolerances, color profile and proof; leave them unknown until provided. Review rendered shelf and unboxing contexts when available. Do not present a brief as a production-ready print file.
