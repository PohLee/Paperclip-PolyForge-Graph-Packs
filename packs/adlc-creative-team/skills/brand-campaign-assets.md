# Brand campaign assets

Start from an accepted brand baseline and a named audience, campaign claim, and touchpoint list. Keep claims sourced, adapt composition and length per channel, and preserve voice and identity. Deliver dimensions/crop guidance, exact copy variants, alt text, asset sources, and license notes. Do not imply that creating assets published them.

## Procedure and completion

1. Build a channel matrix with audience, objective, approved claim, placement, dimensions, safe areas, copy limits and owner. Flag missing facts or licenses before production.
2. Derive one coherent campaign direction from the accepted identity, then adapt hierarchy and crops to each channel rather than resizing blindly. Keep product facts and required disclosures consistent.
3. Produce authorized assets through available host capabilities. Inspect exported files at delivery size for cropping, readable copy, contrast, brand continuity and missing assets.
4. Package editable sources when required, final exports, copy, alt text, rights notes and exact baseline references. Mark unproduced variants and capability gaps explicitly.

Done when the requested asset matrix has reviewed outputs or explicit blocked entries. Generation does not authorize publication, media spend or a brand baseline change.
