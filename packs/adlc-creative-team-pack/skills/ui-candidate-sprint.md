# UI candidate sprint

Use after product design discovery when a promoted wireframe direction needs comparable, implementation-relevant interface alternatives. These are product UI candidates with states, not static visual-concept images and not a complete multi-page website.

## Work

1. Pin the exact product direction, brief, requirements and design baseline. A selected G03 visual concept may guide expression; retain its separate reference and do not treat it as a product-flow decision.
2. Select one or both promoted directions. Create four candidates for each selected direction at comparable fidelity, including key states, accessibility and layout behavior.
3. Freeze each candidate under an immutable version and digest before review.
4. Ask Design QA & Accessibility to inspect every frozen candidate independently against the same brief, baseline, states and criteria. Candidates that changed after review need fresh review.
5. Ask the Design Researcher to shortlist at most two candidates from the frozen candidates that passed review. Keep rejected candidates and reasons.
6. Ask the Design Director to mark the set ready, revision-required or incomplete, then freeze one `ui_candidate_set` with exact candidate, review and decision references.

## Complete when

The set contains at least two comparable candidates for each selected direction and no more than two finalists. Every finalist points to a frozen candidate and accepted independent review. The resulting set may enter the optional prototype experiment or a separate implementation/expansion workflow.
