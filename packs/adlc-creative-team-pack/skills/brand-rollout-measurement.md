# Brand rollout and measurement

In `brand-rollout-plan`, pin the adopted baseline, audience, launch type, owners and channels. Plan internal enablement first, then asset readiness, outward messaging, channel sequencing, customer transition, dependencies and rollback contacts. Handoff to the host release workflow for execution; a completed plan does not publish or certify a release.

Define a small measurement plan with source, collection method, baseline date, cadence and owner for awareness, consideration, preference or retention measures that serve the stated goal. Separate leading indicators from lagging outcomes and state attribution limits. Brand health reports require observed data; do not infer causal ROI from traffic changes alone.
