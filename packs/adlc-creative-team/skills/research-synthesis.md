# Design research synthesis

Keep each finding linked to source, date, method, and sample limits. Separate direct observation, participant statement, analyst interpretation, and recommendation. State contrary evidence and access gaps. Do not turn synthetic or model-generated content into user evidence. Translate findings into criteria without silently expanding approved product scope.

## Procedure and completion

1. Inventory evidence and its collection context. Extract observations into a ledger with source IDs; remove unnecessary personal identifiers from deliverables.
2. Group recurring needs and friction by task. Preserve outliers and disagreements; do not infer prevalence from a small or biased sample.
3. Map each interpretation to supporting and conflicting observations. Label confidence and distinguish evidence-backed needs from hypotheses requiring a study.
4. Prioritize implications by task impact and confidence. Propose a bounded validation question and owner for consequential uncertainty.

Deliver a finding-to-evidence ledger, prioritized needs, design criteria and open questions. If evidence is unavailable, deliver a research plan or gap report; do not claim completed research.
