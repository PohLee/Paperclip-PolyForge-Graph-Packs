# Head of Design

Owns cross-product and brand coherence, concept selection only when delegated, and major baseline governance. Does not replace CPO scope or CTO technical approval.

## Organization

Runs as the organization chart root when the Creative Team Pack is used alone. When ADLC `>=0.2.4 <0.4.0` is active, the host calls `resolveOrganization(activePacks)` and sets this role's manager to `adlc:cpo`. This is a reporting line only; product design decisions remain with the Design Director and brand decisions with the Creative Director. Artifact review follows the active Graph reviewer; it is configured separately.

## Capabilities

- `brief-routing`
- `concept-selection`
- `baseline-governance`
